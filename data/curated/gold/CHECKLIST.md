# MI-CFD-HW checklist - rater instructions

Score each item for each hardware entity using ONLY the text in texts/<entity>.txt.
Question for every item: does the text give this information about the environment the LIVE SPECIMENS experience inside this hardware during flight (or the hardware itself)?
Ignore laboratory preparation, storage, fixation, analysis, ground-lab conditions and pre-flight culture conditions.

Scores: 1 = explicit and quantitative (a number with unit, or an explicit categorical statement where that is all the item needs, e.g. 'sealed canister', 'grown in complete darkness');
0.5 = qualitative only (mentioned without a value, or only a standard envelope such as 'middeck locker'/'EXPRESS rack drawer' for dimensions, or a yes/no flag);
0 = absent.

- **G1 Enclosure dimensions** (critical)
- **G2 Specimen-chamber volume or dimensions** (critical)
- **G3 Specimen layout / number of units**
- **F1 Ventilation mode (forced / passive / sealed)** (critical)
- **F2 Airflow rate or velocity** (critical)
- **E1 Temperature at specimen** (critical)
- **E2 Relative humidity**
- **E3 Gas composition / CO2-O2 control**
- **E4 Pressure**
- **E5 Lighting (type, flux or darkness)**
- **B1 Specimen types** (critical)
- **B2 Nutrient / water delivery**
- **B3 Specimen gas-exchange / metabolic load**
- **T1 Heat load / power**
- **W1 Wall / membrane gas permeability**

Item notes:
- G1: external or internal dimensions of the hardware/specimen enclosure (3 dimensions, or L x W x H). Standard envelope only -> 0.5.
- G2: volume or dimensions of the chamber/container the specimens sit in (e.g. '60 mm Petri dish', '2 L chamber').
- G3: how many specimen units and how they are arranged (e.g. '6 plant pillows', '20 mice in two cages').
- F1: is the specimen air space ventilated by fans/forced air, passively exchanged, or sealed? Explicit statement -> 1.
- F2: numeric airflow rate or air velocity (or water flow rate for aquatic habitats).
- E1: temperature the specimens experience (number).  E2: relative humidity (number).  E3: CO2/O2 composition or control (number or named control method such as a LiOH scrubber -> 1; mention only -> 0.5).
- E4: pressure.  E5: light: intensity/spectrum/photoperiod, or explicit darkness -> 1; lights mentioned -> 0.5.
- B1: specimen organism types.  B2: how specimens get nutrients/water (agar, arcillite, food bars, medium...).  B3: specimen gas-exchange or metabolic rate (number -> 1, mention -> 0.5).
- T1: heat load or electrical power of the hardware (number).  W1: wall/membrane gas permeability (gas-permeable membrane, filter -> 1; wall material only -> 0.5).
