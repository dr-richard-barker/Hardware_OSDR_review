# Data and figure licence

Curated hardware specifications (`hardware/`), model definitions (`models/`), derived
tables (`data/derived/`, `results/tables/`), figures, animations, reduced CFD fields and
the manuscript text are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International licence (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The raw snapshot in `data/raw/` reproduces public NASA data (SPLASH, the OSDR hardware
registry and the OSDR Biological Data API) as retrieved on 2026-10-12; it remains
subject to NASA's data policies. SPLASH asks users to acknowledge: "Data were obtained
from NASA's Space Life Sciences Archive of Scientific Hardware (SPLASH)".
Quotations in `hardware/*.yaml` are short verbatim excerpts from the cited sources,
included as evidence for each value.
